# 🏨 Hotel Reservation Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing hotel reservation data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand booking patterns, customer behaviour, cancellation trends and booking value.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to transform raw reservation data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps identify factors affecting cancellations, understand customer preferences and support better hotel management decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall hotel booking performance.
- Identify booking and cancellation trends.
- Understand customer behaviour and preferences.
- Evaluate booking patterns across room types and market segments.
- Analyze estimated booking value and stay duration.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve hotel operations and customer retention.

## ⚙️ Methodology

1. **Data Understanding:** Examined the dataset structure, columns and booking information.
2. **Data Preparation:** Checked the dataset and created additional calculated columns for analysis.
3. **Calculated Columns:** Created fields such as Total Guests, Total Nights, Guest Category, Stay Category, Month Name and Booking Value.
4. **Pivot Table Analysis:** Summarized booking performance, cancellations, customer segments and booking value.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive dashboards using Pivot Charts, KPI cards and slicers.
6. **Insight Generation:** Identified important patterns and developed practical business recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Insights

**1. Booking Cancellations**
- Approximately 32.76% of total bookings were canceled.
- Online bookings recorded 8,475 cancellations, making them the largest contributor to cancellation volume.

**2. Booking Lead Time**
- Canceled bookings had an average lead time of 139.22 days.
- The overall average lead time was 85.23 days.
- This indicates that bookings made further in advance deserve closer monitoring.

**3. Room Type Performance**
- Room Type 1 recorded the highest booking volume with 28,130 bookings.
- It also recorded 9,072 cancellations.
- Room Type 6 showed a comparatively higher cancellation rate despite its lower booking volume.

**4. Monthly Booking Trend**
- October recorded the highest number of bookings with 5,317.
- September followed with 4,611 bookings.
- January recorded the lowest booking volume with 1,014 bookings.

**5. Market Segment Analysis**
- Online channels contributed the highest booking volume with 23,214 bookings.
- Corporate bookings recorded a comparatively lower cancellation rate of approximately 10.9%.

**6. Customer Retention**
- Only 930 bookings were identified as repeat guest bookings.
- This highlights an opportunity to improve customer loyalty and encourage returning customers.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following improvement strategies are suggested:

- **Reduce Cancellations:** Introduce clear cancellation policies, automated reminders and suitable advance-payment options.
- **Improve Booking Security:** Reconfirm long-lead-time reservations and consider deposits for selected high-demand dates.
- **Optimise Room Performance:** Investigate cancellation patterns and guest feedback for room types with higher cancellation rates.
- **Prepare for Peak Demand:** Plan staffing, housekeeping and room availability ahead of September and October.
- **Strengthen Corporate Relationships:** Develop corporate packages and long-term business partnerships.
- **Improve Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty rewards, personalised offers and returning-guest discounts.
